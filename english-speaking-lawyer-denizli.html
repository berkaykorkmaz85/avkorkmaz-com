Do you need a lawyer in Denizli or elsewhere in Turkey and prefer to communicate in English? Korkmaz Law Office provides legal services to foreign nationals and to Turkish citizens living abroad who have legal matters in Turkey. Based in Denizli, we handle property, inheritance, family, debt-collection and contract matters — and, in most cases, you do not need to travel to Turkey.

This page explains what we do, how we can act on your behalf through a power of attorney, and how the process works remotely.

Who we help

  • Foreign nationals who own or are buying property in Turkey, or who face a legal issue here.
  • Turkish citizens living abroad (in Germany, the Netherlands, Austria, France, Belgium, Switzerland and beyond) with inheritance, property, family or debt matters back home.
  • Foreign companies and investors needing contract drafting/review or dispute support in Turkey.

Practice areas

  • Real estate & property: purchase and title (tapu) transfer, title-cancellation and registration disputes, tenancy and eviction, unjust-occupation compensation.
  • Inheritance: certificate of inheritance, transfer of estate, division of jointly-owned property, disputes among heirs.
  • Family & divorce: divorce, alimony, custody and property division; recognition and enforcement of foreign court judgments in Turkey.
  • Debt collection & enforcement: recovering debts from parties in Turkey, enforcement proceedings.
  • Contracts: drafting and review of lease, service, non-disclosure (NDA), freelance and distribution agreements.

Working remotely (power of attorney)

Under Turkish law, a lawyer holding your power of attorney can file and pursue cases, attend hearings, and complete title and public-office procedures on your behalf. You can issue this power of attorney at a Turkish Consulate in your country, or before a local notary with an apostille and a sworn translation. After that, most steps are handled electronically (Turkey's UYAP system, e-hearings), and you are kept informed by phone, e-mail and video call — with scheduling that respects your time zone.

Why a local lawyer in Denizli?

Property, inheritance and family matters are resolved before the courts and land registries where the assets are located. A lawyer familiar with the Denizli courthouses and local practice helps set the case up correctly, avoids delays, and spares you unnecessary travel. We combine local experience with clear, English-language communication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to travel to Turkey?

Usually not. With a power of attorney, we can act on your behalf for most matters — filing and pursuing cases, attending hearings and completing official procedures.

How do I issue a power of attorney abroad?

You can issue it at a Turkish Consulate in your country, or before a local notary with an apostille and a sworn Turkish translation. We tell you in advance exactly what powers the document should contain.

Is my foreign divorce valid in Turkey?

For a foreign judgment to take effect in Turkey, a "recognition and enforcement" action is required. We can handle this on your behalf.

Can we communicate in English?

Yes. We correspond and hold consultations in English, by e-mail, phone or video call.

Have a legal matter in Turkey?

Let's assess your situation and discuss how we can act on your behalf. We arrange calls across time zones.
